# Departures from REGISTRATION_R1_20261026.md

Each entry is dated and gives the reason. "Clarification" means that the registration left a detail open and the entry records how it was settled before the result was seen.

1. **26/10/2026, Section 1a, EngageNet (change of source, not of method).** Participant-clustered (subject-clustered) intervals for every EngageNet split had already been computed on 4 September 2026, with the same pairing and 2,000 replicates, and stored in `results/E2_results.json` beside the recording-clustered intervals of record. They are reported from that file; nothing was recomputed and no EngageNet label file was read. The file also holds the test-split row, which Section 0 allows because it is a stored interval, not a new read.
2. **26/10/2026, Section 1b (narrower check).** The registration asked for the estimator check on "the participant and recording counts of each split". It was run on the structures of the two training splits only (DAiSEE Train, EngageNet train), with three replicates per true ICC. The validation structures were not simulated.
3. **26/10/2026, Sections 3, 4B–4F (clarification).** "Imported unchanged" metric functions: `eta2_between`, `within_person_auc`, `rho_between` and `point_stats` are copied verbatim from `release_lip_audit/code/p5_person_variance_ci.py` rather than imported. The server copy of that script predates the label-free release and imports `label_join`, which is absent there. The text of the functions is identical.
4. **26/10/2026, Sections 4C–4F (clarification).** "Fixed at the median validation-selected epoch": each run uses the loop of `p4_train_of.py` with its 80-epoch cosine schedule and stops after the epoch whose 0-based index equals the median `best_epoch` of the family's three stored runs. The medians are MLP 3, LSTM 4, CNN–LSTM 1, TCN 6 and Transformer 9. Each run is therefore the checkpoint the original loop would have selected at that epoch.
5. **26/10/2026, Section 4A (clarification).** The latent state runs over every integer position between the first and the last labelled clip of a recording, so that a gap in the position field carries the corresponding number of switch opportunities. H3 is evaluated on the replicate means of each cell.
6. **27/10/2026 07:47 +0700, Section 4G (added runs, decided before any of them started).** The registered A50u run (seed 0) finished on 26/10 without learning. From the first epoch its validation outputs were identical for every clip, and they equalled the training-label prior. Its training loss stayed at 0.867, against a prior entropy of 0.866. The inputs were checked: the 50 frames differ across clips and are not blank. That run is the registered primary outcome and is reported as it stands. Its accuracy equals the majority-class floor. Its within-person AUC₂₃ and ρ_b are undefined on tied scores, so H11 cannot be evaluated on it. This is reported as not evaluable, not as not supported.
   - **Added runs:** A50u with seeds 1 and 2, the recipe otherwise unchanged (same frames, code, optimiser, batch size and 100 epochs). They were added to tell a seed-specific failure from a failure of the recipe at 50 frames.
   - **Stop rule, fixed now:** after epoch 0, a seed whose validation outputs have an across-clip standard deviation below 10⁻⁴ for every class is stopped and recorded as collapsed. Seed 0 showed no recovery over 100 epochs.
   - **Reporting:** every seed is reported, whatever it shows. No seed is chosen on its result, and the pooling, learning rate and other settings are not changed.
   - **H11 for the added seeds:** evaluated for each seed that trains, against the registered band.
   - **Outcome (27/10/2026, about 08:05 +0700):** both added seeds met the stop rule after their first epoch. Their largest across-clip standard deviations were 6.7×10⁻⁶ and 5.5×10⁻⁶. H11 cannot be evaluated on any 50-frame run.
7. **27/10/2026, Section 4B (correction of a count in the registration, logged after the result).** The registration gave "91 participants" for the semi-synthetic condition. That is the number of EngageNet training participants. The decisive axis (labels 2 and 3) covers 85 of them, with 5,398 chunks, and the script used all 85, as its output file records (`n_participants`). Nothing was re-run; the count is corrected in the caption of Table S26.
8. **27/10/2026, Section 1b (estimator, logged after the results during the final pre-submission check).** The registration specified adaptive Gauss–Hermite quadrature for the cumulative-logit mixed model. `r1_label_analyses.py` integrates on a fixed grid of 1,201 points over [−15, 15] instead. The estimator check of Table S22 used the same integrator, and its replicate means lie within .015 of the true ICC in every cell. Nothing was re-run; the integrator is stated in S11.2 and listed in S11.11.
9. **27/10/2026, Section 4E (reporting place, logged after the results).** The leave-one-out constant-per-subject oracle was registered to appear "in the same table" as the controlled conditions. It is reported where it already stood, in Section V-A of the main text and Supplementary S4, and not in Table 9.
10. **27/10/2026, Section 4C, H7 (score definition, logged after the results).** The registration says "between-person share of scores" without naming the score. The paper reports H7 on the ordinal score (posterior mean level), the score of record of Table S15; on that score H7 holds for the MLP and not for the TCN. On the decisive score p3/(p2+p3), also computed by the same script, both intervals include zero: MLP +.026 [−.032, .088], TCN +.013 [−.067, .090]. Both are now reported in S11.7.
